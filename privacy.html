Privacy Policy

Effective: May 15, 2026 · Last updated: May 15, 2026

The Katalys Group ("we," "us") operates Quorum Civic (the "app"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are. We've kept it plain.

Short version: We collect the minimum we need to run the app. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. Your civic interests are not a product.

What we collect

Account information

If you create an account, we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, a unique identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

Usage analytics

We collect anonymized, aggregated analytics about how the app is used — screens viewed, features tapped, errors encountered. This helps us fix bugs and improve the app. We do not record the specific content of your searches or your civic interests in a way that personally identifies you.

Technical information

When you use the app, we automatically receive standard technical information your device sends: device model, iOS version, app version, language preference, and approximate region (derived from IP address, not GPS).

What we do not collect

  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Financial information, brokerage credentials, or trading history
  • Health, biometric, or political party affiliation data

How we use what we collect

  • To run the app: authentication, syncing your bookmarks across devices, sending you alerts you've opted into.
  • To improve the app: understanding which features are used, finding and fixing bugs, measuring performance.
  • To communicate: responding when you contact support, sending occasional product updates if you've opted in.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it to anyone, ever.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run the app. Each one only receives the information needed for its specific function:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • PostHog — anonymized product analytics.
  • Apple — iOS app distribution and crash reporting (via TestFlight and the App Store).
  • Cloud hosting providers — for our backend servers.

Each provider has its own privacy practices. We choose providers that we believe meet a high standard, but their policies apply when your data is on their systems.

Public-record data

Quorum Civic displays information about elected officials, legislation, votes, and corporate filings. This information comes from public government sources (Congress.gov, the Federal Register, the Federal Election Commission, the SEC). It is not information about you. Our use of public-record data is independent of how we handle your account data described above.

Your rights

Access and deletion

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account and all associated data, at any time. Email privacy@thekatalysgroup.com from the address on your account. We'll respond within 30 days.

Opt-out

You can disable analytics in the app settings. You can unsubscribe from any optional emails using the link at the bottom of those emails. You can sign out at any time, which stops new data from being associated with your account.

California, EU, and other jurisdictions

If you are a resident of California, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with specific data-protection laws, you may have additional rights under those laws (including the right to portability and the right to object to certain processing). You can exercise any of these rights by emailing privacy@thekatalysgroup.com.

Data retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active, plus a short period afterward in case you want to restore it. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

Children

Quorum Civic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact privacy@thekatalysgroup.com and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices — encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest, role-based access control on our backend, and routine audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and we'll notify you promptly if we ever discover a breach affecting your account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, give you notice in the app or by email before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
