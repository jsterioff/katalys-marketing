Delete your QuorumCivic account

Last updated July 21, 2026

You can delete your account and its data directly in the app: You tab → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes your account record, follows, and personal data from our systems. Minimal records we are required to keep (payment and security audit records) are retained as described in our Privacy Policy.

No longer have the app installed? Email qcsupport@quorumcivic.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. We honor deletion requests regardless of where you live.

Data stored only on your device (your ZIP-derived district, selected issues) is removed by deleting the app.
