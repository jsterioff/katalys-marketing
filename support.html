Frequently asked

Is QuorumCivic free?

Yes. The core app is free to download and use, including bill search, roll-call votes, and a limited number of new AI bill analyses each month. An optional Quorum Supporter subscription ($3.99/month or $34.99/year) unlocks unlimited AI bill analysis and other Supporter features. The core civic lookup will always be free.

Where does the data come from?

All data in QuorumCivic comes from public sources: Congress.gov for legislation and votes, the Federal Register for executive actions, the Federal Election Commission for campaign finance, and SEC filings for related corporate disclosures. We do not use any proprietary or non-public data.

How fresh is the data?

Most data is refreshed daily. Roll-call votes typically appear within 24 hours of a vote. Bills update as they move through the legislative process. Each record in the app shows when it was last updated.

Which Congress does QuorumCivic cover?

Today, QuorumCivic covers the current Congress, the 119th (2025–2027), so you can't browse earlier sessions yet. Coverage expands over time as new Congresses convene.

Is this financial advice?

No. QuorumCivic is a nonpartisan civic information tool. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or financial guidance of any kind. See our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You tab → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes your account record, follows, and personal data. No longer have the app installed? Email qcsupport@quorumcivic.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. See Account deletion for full details.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Most of QuorumCivic works without an account. An account is only required if you want to save bookmarks, set up alerts, or sync across devices.

Found a bug or have a feature request?

We'd love to hear from you. Email qcsupport@quorumcivic.app with the details. Be as specific as you can. Screenshots help.
