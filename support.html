Frequently asked

Is Quorum Civic free?

Yes. The current version is free to download and use. We may introduce optional paid features in the future, but the core civic lookup will remain free.

Where does the data come from?

All data in Quorum Civic comes from public sources: Congress.gov for legislation and votes, the Federal Register for executive actions, the Federal Election Commission for campaign finance, and SEC filings for related corporate disclosures. We do not use any proprietary or non-public data.

How fresh is the data?

Most data is refreshed daily. Roll-call votes typically appear within 24 hours of a vote. Bills update as they move through the legislative process. Each record in the app shows when it was last updated.

Is this financial advice?

No. Quorum Civic is a civic information tool. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or financial guidance of any kind. See our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@quorumcivic.app from the address on your account and we'll permanently delete your profile and any associated data within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation when this is complete.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Most of Quorum Civic works without an account. An account is only required if you want to save bookmarks, set up alerts, or sync across devices.

Found a bug or have a feature request?

We'd love to hear from you. Email support@quorumcivic.app with the details. Be as specific as you can — screenshots help.
